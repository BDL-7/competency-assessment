Competency Assessment Tracker Presentation

1. For normal viewing or sharing, open competency-assessment-system-audience.html in Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, or another modern browser.
2. For presenting with speaker notes, open competency-assessment-system-presenter.html. Press N to show or hide the notes panel.
3. Use the Left/Right arrow keys or Space to move through slides. Press F for fullscreen.

Both HTML files are self-contained and do not require installation or an internet connection.
