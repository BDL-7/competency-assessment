[S1] Purpose and limitations
This guide describes a fictional Non-CLIA software demonstration. It cannot
establish competency requirements, pass/fail decisions, compliance, or permission
to perform testing. For actual laboratory requirements, use authorized current
guidance reviewed by the source owner. Do not infer missing requirements.

[S2] Fictional reference catalog
SYN-L1-SYSTEM is a fictional test system. Its fictional grouping is SYN-L1-PROCESS.
The demonstration catalog is not the Approved Test Systems catalog.
Other fictional lab IDs follow the same naming pattern for labs 2 through 5.
No actual instrument instructions or equipment manuals are included.

[S3] Assessment fields and evidence
The software example records assessment context, type, process, method, specimen
and testing context, problem-solving evidence, assessor-recorded outcome,
comments and follow-up. Fictional evidence describes a software scenario only.
The assessor records outcomes; the assistant does not determine competency.
An unresolved gap is not proof of completion. The example preserves version 1
with missing follow-up and version 2 with documented fictional follow-up.
This is not a reproduction of the approved assessment form.

[S4] Roles and access
Testing Personnel, Assigned Team Lead, Assessor and Technical Supervisor are
separate participant relationships. A roster role label grants no application
access. Access depends on explicit application role and scope assignments.
Administrative access does not confer assessment or signing authority.
Service identities are counted separately from the 70 fictional humans.

[S5] Workflow, signatures and amendments
The synthetic service scenario progresses through documentation, follow-up,
Testing Personnel signature, Technical Supervisor finalization, locking and
renewal. Signatures bind to one assessment version, not all future versions.
An amendment preserves the earlier version rather than overwriting it.
Dataset signatures are fictional snapshots, not legally valid signatures.
The existing local UI remains read-only and cannot sign or finalize records.

[S6] Dates, history and projections
The pack uses 2026-11-13 as its reference date. Historical example years are
2022 through 2026. Projected years are 2027 through 2031, kept in a separate
planning file. Projections are not completed assessments, signatures or events.
The reused 12 calendar month setting is a fictional test setting only, not a
laboratory requirement. Official renewal dates require approved configuration
and deterministic application calculation, never a chatbot guess.

[S7] Navigation and reporting
The existing pilot has an overview, work queue, assessment detail and issued
history, plus a separate competency guide. Its original one-record seed remains
available. Search, filters and record visibility depend on the configured persona.
The broader pack is loaded separately; it does not add editing, exports or
signing UI. An empty scoped queue is not evidence that records were deleted.

[S8] Notifications, attachments and audit

metadata and hashes, not real evidence or public storage links. Audit snapshots
are fictional; they do not prove that the historical actions actually occurred.
